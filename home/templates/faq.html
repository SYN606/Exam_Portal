Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about taking exams, tracking scores, or technical issues? Find quick answers right here.

Navigate to the "Exams" page from the main menu, choose the test you want to attempt, and click "Start Exam". Make sure you have a stable internet connection before beginning timer-controlled exams.
When the countdown timer reaches zero, your answers will be automatically submitted by the system. Any questions you answered prior to expiration will be saved and evaluated.
You can view all past exam submissions by clicking on your account profile dropdown and selecting Exam History. From there, click "Review" next to any exam to see a question-by-question breakdown.
Retake permissions depend on individual exam configurations set by administrators. Practice tests generally allow multiple retakes, while formal evaluations may limit submissions to a single attempt.
Do not close your browser. Re-establish your internet connection and refresh the page. Your progress is autosaved continuously during active test sessions.
If you spot a typo or incorrect option, visit our Contact Us page, select the "Exam Related Issue" category, and mention the Exam Title & Question Number.
